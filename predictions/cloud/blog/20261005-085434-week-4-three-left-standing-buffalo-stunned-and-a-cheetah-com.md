Week 4 is (almost) in the books — just ATL @ NO left on Monday night — and it was a week that thinned the herd at the top and blew up a bunch of favorites. Let's get into it.

## The last unbeatens

Only **three teams remain undefeated at 4-0**:

- **San Francisco (4-0)** — quietly the most complete team in the league so far.
- **Minnesota (4-0)** — still winning games with defense and duct tape at QB; the Kyler Murray injury hasn't derailed them.
- **Kansas City (4-0)** — remember three weeks ago when we asked if the Chiefs were "back in championship mode"? The model said believe the division; the Chiefs said believe all of it.

And the other end of the table is ugly: **five winless teams (0-4)** — HOU, LAC, TB, TEN, and **Miami**. Hold that last one; it matters in a minute.

## Upset city

Favorites got torched. The market lost **six** games outright, headlined by a genuine stunner:

- **New England 29, Buffalo 26.** The Bills were **−345 favorites at home** — about a 78% lock — and lost. Upset of the week, not close.
- **Carolina 32, Detroit 26** (DET −225) — the Panthers keep ruining someone's Sunday.
- **Dallas 34, Houston 30** — the 0-4 Texans find new ways to lose.
- **Cleveland 27 - Pittsburgh 24**, **NY Giants 36 - Arizona 24**, and **Jacksonville 22 - Cincinnati 17** rounded out the chalk carnage.

If you faded the public this week, you ate well.

## Will Tyreek Hill re-sign with KC?

Here's the story tying it together. Miami **released Tyreek Hill back in February**, and he's spent 2026 rehabbing a serious knee injury from last season — he's reportedly targeting a **November return**. The Dolphins who let him walk? **0-4.** The team that drafted him in the 5th round back in 2016? **4-0.**

And now the reunion chatter is real. After Sunday's 30-27 win over the Raiders cost Kansas City **both Rashee Rice (hamstring) and Tyquan Thornton (dislocated ankle)** — Thornton out indefinitely after a 111-yard, 2-TD day — the Chiefs' receiver room is suddenly thin. Per reporting from The Athletic and ESPN, Kansas City is **"very interested"** and has "remained in contact" with Hill and his agent, and Mahomes, Kelce, and Chris Jones are said to be all for it.

**My read:** the fit is obvious and the timing's almost too perfect — a contender with a WR hole, a healthy-by-November free-agent speed demon who already knows the building, and a locker room recruiting him. The only real questions are the knee and the money. If Hill proves the burst is back, I'd bet on a Cheetah homecoming before Thanksgiving. File it under "watch this space."
