Big news reshuffled the week: **Jalen Hurts is out for the season.** We've updated the model to reflect it, and it changes the Eagles' outlook in a hurry. Plus a Thursday-night Bears win, three teams still perfect, and two still chasing their first win. Let's get into it.

## Jalen Hurts is out for the season

The Eagles just lost their franchise quarterback for the year. **Kenny Pickett** steps in as the starter, and that's a massive downgrade under center — exactly the kind of mid-season shock the model has to be *told* about, because the box score of an upcoming game doesn't exist yet.

So we told it. With Hurts in, the model was **pounding the table on Philadelphia** — it had the Eagles at **~55%** to beat Dallas on Monday night, a "QB rating → PHI" edge that was the single biggest model-vs-market disagreement on the board. We even had the Eagles as this week's paper play.

With Hurts out and Pickett penciled in, the model recalculates to **PHI ~48%** — a coin flip that now tilts slightly toward *Dallas*. The whole thesis flipped: the quarterback edge that made Philadelphia the week's darling is gone. One injury, and our favorite play of the week turned into a shrug. That's the point of keeping a QB in the model as its own dial — pull it, and everything downstream moves.

(Fair warning on the "edge" you'll still see next to this game on the Preview tab: our market number is the line from *before* the news broke. That line is going to crater once the books digest it, so don't read the leftover gap as value — the real market will catch down to where the model now lives.)

## Bears pounce on Thursday night

**Chicago handled New England on Thursday Night Football** to climb to **5-2** and move into the thick of the NFC playoff picture. The Patriots drop to 4-3.

Confession: the model *didn't* love the Bears here — our forecast leaned New England on Elo, and the market agreed. Chicago didn't care what either of us thought. Forecaster, not oracle — we take the L and move on.

## The last three standing

Even with a loss shaking loose almost every week, **three teams are still perfect** heading into Sunday:

- **San Francisco (6-0)** — the most complete roster in football and our runaway Super Bowl favorite at **~23%**. The Niners are road favorites at Atlanta, and the model likes them *even more* than the market does (+16%, below).
- **Minnesota (5-0)** — still winning ugly, still winning. Hosts Indianapolis on Sunday.
- **Kansas City (5-0)** — a Sunday-night trip to Seattle is the week's toughest unbeaten test; the model makes that one close to a coin flip.

Our playoff sim still has the Niners out front, with **Buffalo (~18%)**, **Minnesota (~12%)** and **Kansas City (~8%)** behind them.

## Chasing zero: Chargers and Dolphins

At the other end of the standings, two teams are still searching for their first win:

- **Los Angeles Chargers (0-6)** — the league's biggest disappointment relative to preseason hype. The sim gives them a **~0.1% shot** at the playoffs — effectively drawing dead.
- **Miami (0-5)** — equally buried, but here's the twist: the model **isn't ready to bury them.** In fact, winless Miami is now the week's biggest disagreement.

## Where the model disagrees with Vegas

With the Eagles knocked off their perch, here's the recalculated top of the board:

- **MIA @ NYJ** — Model: **MIA ~53%**. Market: **MIA ~33%.** Yes, the 0-5 Dolphins — the model sees a near coin flip where the books see a comfortable Jets hold. The week's biggest gap (+19%).
- **SF @ ATL** — Model: **SF ~73%** vs market **~57%.** We're even higher on the unbeaten Niners than Vegas is (+16%). Sometimes the disagreement is just "they're really good and you're still underrating them."
- **DAL @ PHI (Mon)** — Post-injury, the model has this **a hair toward Dallas (~52%)**, while the pre-news line still lists Philadelphia as a home dog. As noted above, that gap is a line that hasn't moved yet, not a read to trust.

## Paper trade: a locked play meets bad news

The rules, as always: **one** flat 10-unit play per week — the single biggest model-vs-market disagreement, backing the model's side. Forward, out-of-sample, no cherry-picking, and crucially: **the bet is locked when it's placed.**

**Week 7's play was locked Thursday morning: the Philadelphia Eagles** (+21% edge at the time, a +185 price). That was calculated without taking into account that Hurts was out. If the model had known Hurts was out, it never would have landed on Philadelphia — a useful, honest data point about the cost of betting early.

**Week 6 was a miss** — the NY Giants came up short, **-10 units**. Some weeks the signal looks sharp, some weeks it eats a loss.
