Two weeks in, the field is starting to sort itself into the haves and the have-nots — and then Thursday night gave one of the have-nots a lifeline. Let's get into it.

## The 2-0 club is stacked

Eight teams ran their record to 2-0: **BUF, CIN, KC, LV, MIN, PHI, SEA, SF.** A few of them looked the part in Week 2:

- **Seattle 31, Arizona 7** and **San Francisco 35, Miami 13** — the NFC West's two contenders are steamrolling.
- **Buffalo 41, Detroit 31** — a track meet the Bills won anyway.

Meanwhile the 0-2 pile got crowded: **HOU, IND, LAC, MIA, TB, TEN, WAS.** Hold that thought.

## Are the Chiefs back in championship mode?

Sure look like it. Kansas City is **2-0** after handling Denver 31-10 and then surviving a 33-30 track meet with Indianapolis. The model agrees they're the class of their division — it gives KC a **62% shot to win the AFC West** and comfortably back in the playoff picture.

But "host the AFC Championship again"? Pump the brakes just a hair. The seed that gets you the home game is the **#1 seed**, and right now the model has that spoken for by **Buffalo** — the Bills sit at a **46%** chance to lock up the AFC's top seed versus just **8%** for the Chiefs. So the Chiefs look like a division winner and a real contender, but if the AFC title game runs through anywhere in January, the model says it's currently through **Orchard Park**, not Arrowhead. Ask me again in a month.

## Skol, with the backup

The **Vikings are 2-0**, and they're doing it the hard way. Starter **Kyler Murray** is in concussion protocol after taking a shot in Week 1, so **Carson Wentz** drew the Week 2 start — and Minnesota won **9-3.** Nine to three! That's not an offensive clinic, that's a **defense dragging a team to a win** while the QB room duct-tapes itself together. If that unit keeps playing like this, the Vikings can tread water until Murray clears protocol — and a 2-0 cushion earned with your backup is exactly how good seasons survive September.

## Thursday night: the Dirty Birds finally fly

Atlanta walked into Lambeau on a two-game skid and walked out with a **35-14** beatdown of **Jordan Love** and the Packers. First win of the year, and it wasn't close. Love had a night to forget, and I'm here for it. The Falcons are off the schneid at 1-2, and Green Bay drops to 1-2 with some questions of their own.

## So which of the OTHER 0-2 teams breaks through next?

With Atlanta off the list, seven winless teams remain — and the schedule hands us a gift: **Houston @ Indianapolis** is a matchup of two 0-2 squads, so **somebody is guaranteed to get off the mat.** The model leans hard toward the Texans, giving **Houston ~64%** on the road. That's my headline pick for "first win incoming."

The next-best shot belongs to **Tampa Bay**, home against Minnesota — the model makes it essentially a **coin flip (~50%)**, and a Vikings team starting its backup is a beatable spot.

Everyone else is swimming upstream: **Miami** draws the 2-0 Chiefs, **the Chargers** visit the 2-0 Bills, **Washington** hosts red-hot Seattle, and **Tennessee** goes on the road — all underdogs in the model's eyes. If you're waiting on those four, pack a lunch.
